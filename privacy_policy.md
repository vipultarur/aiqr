Welcome to **Ai Qr** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our mobile application. ## 2. Information We Collect and Use

To provide and improve our Service, we may collect various types of information, including:

### A. Personal Information
When you use certain features of the app, such as creating an account or logging in, we may collect:
*   **Account Information:** When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication, we may collect your name, email address, and profile picture.
*   **User Data:** Data you choose to save or sync, which may be stored securely on our cloud database (Firebase Firestore).

### B. Device and Usage Information
*   **Advertising Data:** We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as device identifiers, location data, and usage data) to serve personalized ads.
*   **Usage Logs:** Information such as device type, operating system version, and general app interaction statistics to help us identify and fix crashes or improve app performance.

## 3. App Permissions

To function correctly, Ai Qr requires the following permissions on your device:
*   **Camera Permission:** Required to scan QR codes and barcodes. We process these images locally on your device in real-time. We do not record video or remotely store images from your camera without your explicit consent.
*   **Storage/Photo Gallery Permission:** Required to allow you to select images from your gallery to scan for QR codes, or to save generated QR codes to your device's local storage.
*   **Internet Access & Network State:** Required to authenticate your account, sync your history data, fetch content from scanned URLs, and serve advertisements.
*   **Vibration Control:** Used to provide haptic feedback when a QR code is successfully scanned.
